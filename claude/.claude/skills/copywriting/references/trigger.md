# Triggers for my personas

Trigger is a specific moment that makes them finally act.

## Persona 1 — The Frustrated Mid-Level
Their pain is chronic, but the triggers are acute moments:

- A bug they caused makes it to production and their manager notices
- QA sends back the same ticket for the third time in a sprint
- A senior dev reviews their PR and rewrites half of it
- They try to add a feature and the codebase fights them for days
- A colleague joins and writes noticeably cleaner code than them
- A page freezes in production and they can't explain why

## Persona 2 — The Self-Doubting Senior
Their pain is mostly internal, so triggers tend to be social or professional shocks:

- They get passed over for a tech lead role they expected
- A junior asks them a design question and they can't answer confidently
- They're asked to define the team's testing strategy and freeze
- A new senior joins and clearly knows more about architecture than they do
- Performance review comes and they realize they can't point to real technical growth

## Persona 3 — The Ambitious Grower
They're proactive, so triggers are more goal-driven than reactive:

- They apply for a mid/senior role and fail the technical interview
- They finish yet another course and still can't apply it at work
- They see a peer get promoted or land a better job
- They get assigned to a complex ticket and feel completely out of their depth
- They realize they've been at the same level for 12+ months

## Persona 4 — The Silent Follower
These are the hardest to trigger because fear is their default state. Their triggers are usually permission-based:

- Nik posts something that feels like it was written specifically about them
- The community gets framed as small, safe, and private (the 50-member cap does this)
- Someone they respect publicly says they joined
- A limited-time or limited-seat offer creates urgency without pressure
- Nik personally reaches out or responds to a comment they left
