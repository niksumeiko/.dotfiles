# Personas for my paid community

This is my target niche audience.

## Persona 1 — "The Frustrated Mid-Level"
A React developer with 2–5 years of experience who ships features but knows something is off. Their codebase is getting harder to change, bugs keep appearing after each sprint, and writing tests feels painful. They've heard of Clean Architecture and TDD but haven't been able to apply them in a real project. They're motivated by quality and craft, not just getting things done. Nik's posts about legacy code, brittle tests, and the "zero bugs policy" speak directly to their daily frustrations.

## Persona 2 — "The Self-Doubting Senior"
A developer who holds a senior title but privately feels like they're missing something foundational — especially around software design and testing discipline. They code a lot but skip tests "for now," rely on gut feeling over systems, and wonder why their team still ships bugs. They're drawn to Nik's credibility (Erste Digital, 8 years, 120+ mentees) and respond to posts that validate their experience while showing a clear path forward.

## Persona 3 — "The Ambitious Grower"
A junior-to-mid developer who is deliberately building their career. They're already consuming lots of content (YouTube, LinkedIn, courses) but feel scattered. They want structure, a mentor, and a community of serious people. They responded to Nik's "best growth hacks" posts and the promise of a diploma/certificate that signals credibility to employers. The community's 50-member cap and personal attention are strong hooks for this persona.

## Persona 4 — "The Silent Follower"
Nik actually names this persona directly in Post 132 — someone who has been watching quietly for months, never commented, but reaches out in DMs. They're often introverted, slightly insecure about showing up publicly, and need a safe, private space to learn. The community's framing as protected and friendly is exactly what converts them. They won't come in through aggressive marketing — they need trust built over time.
