# Pain points for my personas

These are emotional pain points for my target personas.

## Persona 1 — The Frustrated Mid-Level

"Every time I add a new feature, something else breaks and I don't know why."
"Writing tests feels slower than just shipping — so I skip them and regret it later."
"I've read about Clean Architecture but I have no idea how to apply it to my actual codebase."
"My code works, but I'm embarrassed to show it to senior devs."
"I spend more time fixing bugs than building new things."
"I know my code is messy but I don't know where to start cleaning it up."
"Every refactor feels like I'm playing Jenga — one wrong move and everything falls."
"QA keeps sending things back to me and it kills my momentum."
"I copy patterns from tutorials but they never quite work in my real project."
"I'm stuck at mid-level and I don't know what 'senior' actually looks like in practice."
"My codebase is a year old and already feels like legacy."
"I can't tell if my architecture decisions are good or just familiar."
"I've tried TDD twice and gave up both times — it just slows me down."
"My PRs get an LGTM in minutes. No one ever questions my decisions."


## Persona 2 — The Self-Doubting Senior

"I have the title but I still ship bugs like a junior."
"I say I'll write tests after the feature is done. I never do."
"I can't confidently explain why I made a design decision — I just did what felt right."
"New devs on the team ask me questions I don't have good answers to."
"I mentor others but I'm not sure I'm teaching them the right things."
"I've been doing React for 5 years but I feel like I've just repeated year one five times."
"My team trusts me, but I feel like an imposter every sprint."
"I know SOLID exists, I just can't connect it to my day-to-day React work."
"I keep saying we need better testing culture but I don't know how to make it stick."
"I've read Clean Architecture. I still don't know what the Repository layer does in React."
"My PRs get approved but I'm never fully proud of what I shipped."
"I avoid refactoring because I'm scared of breaking things with no safety net."
"I want to lead technical decisions but I don't have the vocabulary or confidence yet."


## Persona 3 — The Ambitious Grower

"I'm consuming tons of content but nothing connects into a real learning path."
"I don't know what to learn next — there's too much and everyone says something different."
"I land the job but struggle once the codebase gets complex."
"I want to stand out in interviews but I can't point to anything that makes me different."
"I don't have a senior dev around me to learn from."
"I work alone or in a small team — no one does code reviews or pair programming."
"I want proof of my skills — not just another course certificate no one cares about."
"I watch tutorials but I can't replicate the same quality in my work projects."
"I ask questions online and get 10 conflicting opinions."
"I want to grow fast but I don't want to pick up bad habits that slow me down later."
"I feel behind compared to other developers my age."
"I'm good enough to ship features but not good enough to design a system."
"I've never worked on an enterprise codebase — I don't know what 'real' looks like."
"I want to be taken seriously in a team of seniors, not just the junior who googles everything."
"I studied Big O for interviews, but I never know when it actually matters at work."


## Persona 4 — The Silent Follower

"I follow Nik for months but feel too inexperienced to reach out."
"Public communities feel intimidating — I don't want to ask a 'dumb question' in front of everyone."
"I learn a lot alone but have no one to validate if I'm on the right track."
"I'm afraid that if I show my code, people will judge how bad it is."
"I've invested in courses before and felt abandoned after the first module."
"I want a mentor but I don't know if I'm 'ready enough' to make it worth their time."
"I feel like everyone else already knows this stuff and I'm the only one catching up."
"I don't comment on posts because I don't want to say something wrong publicly."
"I've never had a real conversation with someone who ships production-grade React code."
"I want accountability but I don't know how to ask for it."
"I learn in private but I have no one to discuss ideas with after."
"I'm scared to pay for something and still feel lost."
"I've been meaning to reach out to Nik for three months. I just never felt ready."
"I want to belong to a group of serious developers — I just don't know if I'd fit in."
